Frequently asked questions

Can I sell my house in Fort Myers without repairs?

Possibly. Compare an as-is listing with direct buyer offers, accounting for price, timing, conditions and fees.

What should I do with an inherited Cape Coral property?

Confirm legal ownership and probate requirements with an attorney, then assess condition, carrying costs and sale options.

How can I estimate my home value in Lehigh Acres?

Compare recent relevant closed sales based on condition, square footage, age and location. An estimate is not an appraisal.

Should I take a cash offer or list my home?

Compare likely net proceeds, contingencies and transaction certainty rather than the offer amount alone.
